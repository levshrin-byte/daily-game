Elevation: AWS Terrain Tiles (SRTM and others). Map: Natural Earth, GeoNames.

How to play

  1. Every day brings a new straight line between two real places.
  2. Drag across the chart to sketch how high the ground is along that line, from A on the left to B on the right.
  3. Submit to see the real profile. The closer your line, the higher your score out of 100.

Each square in your result covers a fifth of the route: 🟩 spot on, 🟨 close, 🟧 off, 🟥 way off.

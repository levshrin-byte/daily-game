How to play

  1. Each day there's a new straight line between two real places.
  2. Drag across the chart to sketch how high the ground is along that line, from A on the left to B on the right.
  3. Submit to reveal the real profile. The closer your line, the higher your score (out of 100).

Each square in your result is one fifth of the route: 🟩 spot on · 🟨 close · 🟧 off · 🟥 way off.
